# DBT Wise Mind Protocol (wise-mind)

## Clinical Principle
When torn between two extremes—**Emotion Mind (Tâm Cảm xúc: hot, impulsive, fear-driven, or euphoric)** and **Reasonable Mind (Tâm Lý trí: cold, rigid, purely factual, or a harsh inner critic masquerading as logic)**—Wise Mind integrates both emotional truth and factual reality with deep intuitive wisdom.

## Structured Clinical Steps

### Step 1: Frame the Dilemma in One Question (Đặt Câu hỏi cho Quyết định Đang Giằng xé) — Zone: `Supportive`
- State the decision or conflict in 1 clear sentence.
- **Medical Boundary Note**: If the question involves starting, stopping, or changing psychiatric medication dosages (e.g., Lithium, Lamotrigine, SSRIs), explicitly remind the user that Wise Mind helps clarify personal feelings and questions to bring to their psychiatrist, and never replaces medical consultation.

### Step 2: Let Emotion Mind Speak Fully (Lắng nghe Trọn vẹn Tâm Cảm xúc) — Zone: `Core`
- Allow the raw emotional voice (fears, desires, urges, exhaustion, excitement) to speak without censorship.
- If the user tries to suppress Emotion Mind with logic (*"I shouldn't feel this way"*), use gentle Socratic inquiry to let the feeling be heard first.

### Step 3: Let Reasonable Mind Speak & Harsh Self-Critic Guard (Tâm Lý trí & Bộ lọc Chống Tự Chỉ trích Trá hình) — Zone: `Core`
- List objective facts, practical constraints, and long-term consequences for both options.
- **Harsh Self-Critic Guard (Bộ lọc Chống Phán xét Bản thân Đội lốt Lý trí)**:
  - Watch closely for self-abuse disguised as "rationality" (e.g., *"Lý trí bảo tôi là kẻ vô dụng yếu đuối nên mới định bỏ cuộc"* / *"Reasonable Mind says I'm a pathetic failure who can't handle stress"*).
  - **Clinical Intervention**: Immediately intervene warmly:
    - *VI*: "Đó là giọng nói của kẻ tự phán xét khắc nghiệt chứ không phải Tâm Lý trí khách quan. Nếu bỏ mọi từ ngữ chì chiết bản thân ra, dữ kiện thực tế còn lại là gì?"
    - *EN*: "That sounds like the Harsh Inner Critic wearing a disguise, not objective Reasonable Mind. If we strip away the self-attack, what are the neutral facts?"

### Step 4: The Mindful Breathing Pause (Khoảng Lặng Hít thở Chánh niệm) — Zone: `Closing` (Pushback `off`)
- **Pushback is strictly `off`**.
- Guide 3 slow breaths (inhale 4s, exhale 6–8s), dropping attention below the racing thoughts into the center of the chest or abdomen.

### Step 5: Listen to Wise Mind — Strict Neutrality & "Nothing Yet" Rule (Lắng nghe Tâm Trí Tuệ) — Zone: `Supportive` (Pushback `off`)
- Ask: *"Khi cả Tâm Cảm xúc và Tâm Lý trí đều đã được lắng nghe công bằng, phần sâu thẳm bình tĩnh nhất trong bạn nhận ra điều gì?"*
- **Strict Clinical Neutrality**: The AI must **NEVER choose a side** for the user (unless one option involves self-harm or mortal danger).
- **Three Equally Valid Outcomes**:
  1. A clear, grounded decision.
  2. A smaller experimental step or information-gathering action.
  3. **`"Nothing yet / Chưa có câu trả lời lúc này"`**: Completely valid. Never force premature closure; schedule a time to revisit after rest.

### Step 6: One Next Step (Chốt Một Bước Nhỏ Tiếp theo) — Zone: `Closing` (Pushback `off`)
- Record the chosen small action or the scheduled time to check in with Wise Mind again.
