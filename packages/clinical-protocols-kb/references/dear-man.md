# DBT DEAR MAN + TEAM-CBT EAR Communication & Roleplay Protocol (dear-man)

## Clinical Purpose & Relationship Safety Gate
Helps users draft a concise 4–6 sentence script in their own voice to **make a clear request** or **say No with boundaries**, directly treating the **Hidden Emotion Model of Anxiety** (*"Niceness / Conflict Phobia"*).
- **Mandatory Domestic Abuse / Danger Gate**: If the other person is physically violent, coercive, or dangerous to confront, **immediately abort DEAR MAN negotiation** (`outcome: justified-exit`), turn Pushback `off`, and pivot to physical/emotional safety planning.

## Structured Clinical Steps

### Step 1: Clarify the Ask & Primary Priority (Xác định Mục tiêu & Ưu tiên Cốt lõi) — Zone: `Core`
- Define in 1 sentence what you are asking for (or saying No to), and rank your primary priority:
  1. **Objective Effectiveness (`DEAR MAN`)**: Getting the concrete result.
  2. **Relationship Effectiveness (`GIVE`)**: Preserving warmth and connection.
  3. **Self-Respect Effectiveness (`FAST`)**: Keeping integrity without over-apologizing or betraying values.

### Step 2: D — Describe the Facts Only (Mô tả Sự thật Khách quan Như Máy quay) — Zone: `Core`
- State only observable facts without mind-reading or character attacks.
- Use Socratic Pushback to strip out words like *"You always..."*, *"You never..."*, or *"You don't care about me"*.

### Step 3: E — Express Using "I Feel" Statements (Bày tỏ Cảm xúc theo Cấu trúc "Tôi Cảm thấy...") — Zone: `Supportive`
- Express your own emotion calmly (*"I feel overwhelmed / worried when..."*) rather than blaming (*"You make me feel..."*).

### Step 4: A — Assert Clearly Without Pre-Apologizing (Đưa ra Yêu cầu / Từ chối Trực diện) — Zone: `Core`
- Ask directly for the specific behavioral change or state a clear "No".
- Use Pushback to eliminate self-defeating pre-apologies (*"I'm so sorry to bother you, it's probably stupid, but..."*).

### Step 5: R — Reinforce the Positive Outcome (Củng cố Lợi ích Khi Đối phương Hợp tác) — Zone: `Supportive`
- Highlight the genuine benefit to the other person or the relationship if they agree (never frame as a threat).

### Step 6: M-A-N Delivery Strategy & TEAM-CBT EAR Disarming (Chiến lược Truyền đạt M-A-N & 5 Bí quyết EAR) — Zone: `Supportive`
- **M — Mindful (`Broken Record`)**: Draft 1 calm 10-word sentence to repeat if the other person deflects or baits an argument.
- **A — Appear Confident**: Steady voice, level posture, no apologetic trailing off.
- **N — Negotiate & Turn the Tables**: Define your minimum acceptable compromise, or ask: *"Vậy theo bạn chúng ta có thể giải quyết việc này bằng cách nào?"*
- **TEAM-CBT EAR Integration (When Receiving Criticism)**:
  - **Disarming Technique**: Find the grain of truth in the other person's complaint even if they are exaggerating.
  - **Thought & Feeling Empathy + Inquiry + "I Feel" + Affirmation (Stroking)**.

### Step 7: Assemble Script & Interactive AI Roleplay Rehearsal (Ghép Kịch bản & Tập dượt Đóng vai AI) — Zone: `Closing`
- Combine Steps 2–5 into a natural 4–6 sentence script.
- Offer **1 to 3 rounds of AI Roleplay Rehearsal** with adjustable resistance (`softer`, `moderate`, `harder`). After each exchange, step out of character to give **1 specific strength** and **1 actionable refinement**.
