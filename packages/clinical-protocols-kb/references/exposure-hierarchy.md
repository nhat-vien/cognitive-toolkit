# CBT Exposure Hierarchy, Interoceptive Exposure & Safety Behavior Detector (exposure-hierarchy)

## Clinical Mechanism (Inhibitory Learning & Habituation)
Avoidance and subtle **Safety Behaviors** provide temporary relief (negative reinforcement) that teaches the amygdala: *"You only survived because you escaped or used your crutch!"* Gradual, planned exposure without safety behaviors builds new inhibitory pathways proving that fear peaks and naturally subsides—and that the feared catastrophe either does not occur or is tolerable.

## Structured Clinical Steps

### Step 1: Identify Feared Target & Core Catastrophic Prediction (Xác định Nỗi sợ & Dự đoán Thảm họa) — Zone: `Supportive`
- Define the avoided situation, bodily sensation, or obsessive trigger, and state the specific feared outcome (`0–100%` probability prediction).

### Step 2: Build the SUDS Exposure Ladder (Xây dựng Thang Tiếp xúc SUDS 0–100) — Zone: `Core`
- Break the feared target into 5–10 graded rungs rated by **SUDS (Subjective Units of Distress Scale, `0–100`)**:
  - **Low Rungs (`SUDS 20–40`)**: Starting steps with high willingness.
  - **Medium Rungs (`SUDS 45–65`)**: Moderate challenge.
  - **High Rungs (`SUDS 70–95`)**: Core feared scenarios (including David Burns' *Shame-Attacking* or *Feared Fantasy* exercises for social anxiety).

### Step 3: Interoceptive Exposure & Medical Safety Screen (Tiếp xúc Nội cảm cho Rối loạn Hoảng loạn) — Zone: `Closing` (Pushback `off`)
- For Panic Disorder (fear of bodily sensations like racing heart, dizziness, shortness of breath):
  - **Mandatory Medical Exclusion Screen**: Verify no uncontrolled cardiovascular disease, severe asthma, epilepsy, or pregnancy complications before physical symptom-induction exercises (such as 30s brisk stepping in place, spinning slowly in a chair, or breathing through a straw).
  - Goal: Experience harmless physiological arousal cleanly without escaping, uncoupling bodily sensations from the thought *"I am dying / losing control"*.

### Step 4: Safety Behavior Detector & Elimination Plan (Phát hiện & Gỡ bỏ Hành vi An toàn Ngầm) — Zone: `Core`
- Scan for subtle crutches that sabotage exposure:
  - *Reassurance seeking* (asking others *"Are you mad at me?"*, *"Will I be okay?"* repeatedly)
  - *Compulsive checking* (checking pulse/blood pressure, googling symptoms, re-reading emails 10 times)
  - *Mental rituals / distraction* (scrolling phone to avoid eye contact, sitting only near exits, carrying unnecessary rescue items)
  - *Over-preparation / perfectionism* (rehearsing every sentence before speaking)
- Commit to dropping at least **1 safety behavior** during the upcoming exposure trial.

### Step 5: Execute Exposure & Log Prediction Calibration (Thực hành Tiếp xúc & Đối chiếu Kết quả) — Zone: `Closing`
- Stay in the exposure until SUDS drops significantly or the allotted time completes without safety behaviors.
- Record **Peak SUDS**, **End SUDS**, and **Did the feared catastrophe happen?** to update the user's **Prediction Calibration** score.
