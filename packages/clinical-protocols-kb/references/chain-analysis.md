# DBT Behavioral Chain Analysis & Missing-Links Protocol (chain-analysis)

## Clinical Purpose & Stance: Curiosity, Not Blame
Unpacks a specific regrettable target behavior (e.g., anger outburst, impulsive hypomanic purchase, binge drinking, doomscrolling until 4 AM, or avoiding a crucial commitment via **Missing-Links Analysis**) link by link so the user can design effective skill interventions for next time.

## Mandatory Clinical Hard Stops & Shame Protocol (Cổng Chặn Cứng & Giao thức Bảo vệ Xấu hổ)

### Hard Stop 1: Self-Harm or Suicidal Behavior (Cấm Tuyệt đối Phân tích Chuỗi Tự hại trên App Tự lực)
- **Rule**: NEVER run an automated Chain Analysis on non-suicidal self-injury (cutting, burning, hitting oneself) or a suicide attempt. Analyzing self-harm chains in detail without a live clinician can trigger intense shame and re-arousal/contagion.
- **Action**: Trigger immediate **Hard Stop (`outcome: safety-stop`)**, turn Pushback `off`, validate the user's courage in sharing, provide crisis support / safety plan, and advise working through that specific chain directly with a trained DBT therapist.

### Hard Stop 2: Victimization / Someone Else's Abuse (Cấm Phân tích Chuỗi Khi Người dùng là Nạn nhân)
- **Rule**: NEVER run Chain Analysis when the "target event" is another person abusing, assaulting, cheating on, or harming the user.
- **Action**: Trigger immediate **Hard Stop (`outcome: justified-exit`)**, turn Pushback `off`, state clearly: *"Việc người khác đối xử tệ hoặc xâm hại bạn không phải là hành vi lỗi của bạn để phân tích chuỗi"*, and pivot to safety and boundary support.

### Shame Protocol (Giao thức Dịu nhẹ Khi Xuất hiện Xấu hổ Gay gắt)
- Whenever the user expresses acute self-disgust or shame (*"Tôi thật kinh tởm, ngu ngốc, không hiểu sao mình cứ lặp lại lỗi này"* / *"I am disgusting, stupid, hopeless"*):
  - **Immediately set Pushback to `off`**.
  - Warmly name and normalize the shame, slow the pace, remind the user that every link made sense in context, and offer a pause before continuing.

## Structured Clinical Steps

### Step 1: Define the Specific Target Behavior (Mô tả Chính xác 1 Hành vi Cụ thể) — Zone: `Core`
- Pinpoint **one single episode** (What exact action happened—or didn't happen—when, where, and for how long?), free of vague global labels.

### Step 2: The Prompting Event (Sự kiện Kích hoạt / Giọt nước Tràn ly) — Zone: `Core`
- Identify the exact external or internal event right before things started sliding toward the target behavior.

### Step 3: Vulnerability Factors — DBT PLEASE Scan (Các Yếu tố Làm Suy yếu Sức đề kháng) — Zone: `Supportive`
- Check what made the nervous system extra vulnerable that day:
  - **P**hysical illness or pain
  - **E**ating (skipped meals, blood sugar crash, excess sugar)
  - **A**ltering substances (alcohol, high caffeine, missed mood stabilizer medication)
  - **S**leep debt (`< 6h` sleep, disrupted circadian IPSRT anchors)
  - **E**xercise deficit or accumulated background stress / prior interpersonal friction

### Step 4: The Chain of Links (Giải phẫu Chuỗi Mắt xích 5–10 Bước) — Zone: `Core`
- Reconstruct the micro-steps connecting the Prompting Event to the Target Behavior, tagging each link explicitly:
  - `[thought]` (Suy nghĩ)
  - `[feeling]` (Cảm xúc)
  - `[body sensation]` (Cảm giác cơ thể)
  - `[action / urge]` (Hành động / Thôi thúc)
  - `[external]` (Tác động bên ngoài)

### Step 5: Honest Consequences — Short-Term Relief vs. Long-Term Cost (Hệ quả Ngắn hạn & Dài hạn) — Zone: `Supportive`
- Acknowledge the **Short-Term Payoff** without judgment (e.g., instant relief, numbness, dopamine rush, escaping conflict—this is *why* the brain repeated the behavior) alongside the **Long-Term Cost** to values, health, finances, and relationships.

### Step 6: Break Points, Prevention & Skillful Repair (Điểm Ngắt Chuỗi, Phòng ngừa & Sửa chữa) — Zone: `Core`
- Choose 1–2 early links on the chain and assign a **concrete replacement DBT/CBT skill** (e.g., `TIPP`, `STOP`, `48h Impulse Vault`, `Opposite Action`).
- Define **1 Vulnerability Prevention step** from Step 3 (e.g., protecting sleep/medication) and **1 healthy Repair action** (genuine amends without groveling or excessive self-degradation).
