# TEAM-CBT Paradoxical Agenda Setting (paradoxical-agenda-setting)

## Clinical Principle (Melting Outcome & Process Resistance)
In Dr. David Burns' **TEAM-CBT** (**T**esting → **E**mpathy → **A**genda Setting → **M**ethods), jumping straight to cognitive restructuring often fails because patients harbor subconscious **Outcome Resistance** (secret advantages to keeping anxiety, depression, self-criticism, or guilt) and **Process Resistance** (the hard work Required for recovery). Instead of fighting resistance, we honor it paradoxically so the user sits on the therapeutic side of the table.

## Structured Clinical Steps

### Step 1: The Magic Button Question (Câu hỏi Nút bấm Phép thuật) — Zone: `Supportive`
- Ask warmly: *"Nếu trên bàn có một nút bấm phép thuật mà khi bấm vào, toàn bộ nỗi lo âu / sự tự trách / nỗi buồn này sẽ biến mất hoàn toàn ngay lập tức, bạn có chắc chắn 100% muốn bấm nó không?"*

### Step 2: Positive Reframing — What Your Symptom Says About Your Core Values (Lợi ích & Vẻ đẹp Nhân văn của Triệu chứng) — Zone: `Core`
- Invite the user to list **5 to 8 advantages and noble qualities** shown by their negative emotion:
  - *Anxiety*: Shows I care deeply about my family/work, keeps me vigilant, prevents complacency, feels like a protective shield (*Magical Thinking*).
  - *Self-Criticism / Shame / Guilt*: Proves I have high moral standards, keeps me humble, punishes me before others can criticize me.
  - *Depression / Hopelessness*: Protects me from the pain of trying and failing again, signals that my deepest values are unmet.

### Step 3: The Acid Test (Phép thử Thực tế) — Zone: `Core`
- Gently examine whether the symptom is actually required to keep those noble values:
  - *"Do you really need chronic panic attacks at 2 AM in order to be a loving, responsible parent/professional, or does the panic actually drain the energy you want to give them?"*

### Step 4: The Magic Dial (Chiếc Núm vặn Phép thuật 0–100%) — Zone: `Supportive`
- Never force the user to eliminate an emotion to `0%` (which feels fake or unsafe).
- Ask: *"Nếu nỗi lo âu/buồn bã đang ở mức hiện tại (ví dụ 85%), bạn muốn vặn chiếc núm xuống mức bao nhiêu phần trăm hôm nay để vừa giữ được sự cẩn trọng/trách nhiệm, vừa không bị kiệt sức (ví dụ 25% hoặc 30%)?"*

### Step 5: Transition to TEAM-CBT Recovery Circle Methods (Chuyển sang Kỹ thuật Chuyển hóa) — Zone: `Closing`
- Only after the user freely chooses their target dial level do we unlock active methods (*Externalization of Voices, Double-Standard, Thought Record, Feared Fantasy, Downward Arrow*).
