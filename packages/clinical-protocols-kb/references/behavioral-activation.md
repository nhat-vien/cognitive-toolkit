# CBT Behavioral Activation 6-Step Protocol (behavioral-activation)

## Clinical Principle & Bipolar Pacing Guard
- Depression thrives on the vicious cycle: *Low energy → Withdrawal → Loss of positive reinforcement (Mastery & Pleasure) → Deeper depression*. Action precedes motivation.
- **Bipolar Depression Guard (Chống Chuyển pha Hưng cảm / Manic Switch)**: When a user has Bipolar I/II/Cyclothymia in a depressive pole (`moodPolarity < 0`), cap activation to **Micro-Activation (1 gentle activity/day, 5–15 mins)** and preserve strict sleep/wake IPSRT anchors. Never prescribe high-intensity late-night goal sprints.

## Structured Clinical Steps

### Step 0: Follow-up & Prediction-Error Correction (Đối chiếu Sai số Dự đoán Phiên trước) — Zone: `Supportive`
- Review the previously scheduled activity: compare **Predicted Mastery (M) & Pleasure (P)** (`0–10`) against **Actual Mastery & Pleasure** (`0–10`).
- Highlight behavioral evidence against depressive fortune-telling (*"Depression predicted 0/10 pleasure, yet walking outside for 5 minutes yielded 4/10"*).

### Step 1: What Has Dropped Away (Nhận diện Lĩnh vực Bị Thu hẹp) — Zone: `Supportive`
- Scan 5 life domains: (1) Social Connection, (2) Physical Movement, (3) Personal Hobbies/Play, (4) Sense of Accomplishment/Order, (5) Self-Care & Living Space.

### Step 2: Mandatory Rest Check (Cổng Kiểm tra Nhu cầu Nghỉ ngơi Sinh học) — Zone: `Closing` (Pushback `off`)
- **Critical Clinical Safeguard**: Distinguish **Depressive Avoidance** (withdrawal that leaves the user feeling heavier, stagnant, and guilty) from **Biological Exhaustion** (acute sleep deprivation, physical illness, sensory burnout, bereavement, overwork).
- **Pushback is strictly `off`**.
- If the user genuinely needs biological recovery:
  - Convert the session plan into **Deliberate Guilt-Free Rest (Nghỉ ngơi Chủ động Không Áy náy)** with a defined start time, end time, and permission not to productive-multitask.
  - Close session immediately with `outcome: justified-exit`.

### Step 3: What Matters vs. "Should" Statements (Lọc Giá trị Thật khỏi Mệnh lệnh "Phải/Nên") — Zone: `Core`
- Use Socratic Pushback to separate externalized *"I should/must"* obligations from activities that bring personal meaning, relief, or *"feel slightly less awful this week"* (for severe anhedonia).

### Step 4: One Activity Made Small & Confidence Scaling >= 7/10 (Thu nhỏ Việc làm đến Ngưỡng Tự tin >= 7/10) — Zone: `Core`
- Select **one specific activity** (5–20 minutes) with an exact **Day, Time, and Location**.
- Ask the user to rate confidence (`0–10`) that they can complete it even if their mood stays low:
  - **Confidence Scaling Rule**: If confidence `< 7/10`, **mandatory shrink the task** (e.g., from *"Clean the kitchen"* to *"Wash 3 dishes for 3 minutes"*, or from *"Go to the gym"* to *"Put on shoes and stand on the balcony for 2 minutes"*) until confidence reaches `>= 7/10`.

### Step 5 & Step 6: Predict Mastery/Pleasure & If-Then Barrier Plan (Dự đoán M/P & Kế hoạch Vượt Rào cản Nếu-Thì) — Zone: `Core`
- Record **Predicted Mastery (`0–10`)** and **Predicted Pleasure (`0–10`)**.
- Draft a concrete **If-Then Implementation Intention** assuming low motivation will strike:
  - *VI*: "Nếu đến 16:00 chiều nay mình thấy mệt và không có chút hứng thú nào, THÌ mình sẽ chỉ làm đúng 2 phút đầu tiên rồi cho phép bản thân dừng lại."
  - *EN*: "IF at 4:00 PM today I feel zero motivation and heavy fatigue, THEN I will do only the first 2 minutes and give myself full permission to stop after that."
