# DBT Crisis Pros & Cons 4-Grid Protocol (crisis-pros-cons)

## Clinical Mechanism
When seized by an intense destructive urge (e.g., hypomanic impulse spending, quitting a job in anger, sending a bridge-burning message, drinking/bingeing, or abandoning treatment), the brain hyper-focuses on the **immediate 5-minute relief/thrill** while going blind to the **long-term aftermath**. The DBT 4-Square Matrix forces both timeframes into working memory before action occurs.

## Structured Clinical Steps

### Step 1: Name the Crisis Urge & Run DBT STOP (Gọi tên Xung động & Dừng lại với STOP) — Zone: `Supportive`
- State the exact impulsive action you feel driven to take right now and rate urge intensity (`0–10`).
- Freeze physical action for 60 seconds (**S**top — **T**ake a step back — **O**bserve — **P**roceed mindfully).

### Step 2: Cell 1 & Cell 2 — Acting on the Crisis Urge (Phân tích Ô 1 & Ô 2: Làm theo Xung động Ngay lúc này) — Zone: `Core`
- **Cell 1: Pros of Acting on the Urge (Lợi ích Ngắn hạn)**: Be completely honest—what immediate rush, relief, escape, or dopamine hit is the urge promising for the next 15 minutes?
- **Cell 2: Cons of Acting on the Urge (Cái giá & Hậu quả Dài hạn)**: How will you feel tomorrow morning or next week? What happens to your savings, relationships, self-respect, or bipolar stability?

### Step 3: Cell 3 & Cell 4 — Resisting / Riding Out the Urge for 48 Hours (Phân tích Ô 3 & Ô 4: Chịu đựng Xung động & Chờ 48 Giờ) — Zone: `Core`
- **Cell 3: Pros of Resisting the Urge (Lợi ích Dài hạn của Việc Chờ đợi)**: Keeping financial safety, intact relationships, pride in self-mastery, and a calm morning tomorrow.
- **Cell 4: Cons of Resisting the Urge (Sự Khó chịu Ngắn hạn cần Chịu đựng)**: Sitting with temporary restlessness, FOMO, or frustration for the next few hours.

### Step 4: Commit to the 48-Hour Impulse Vault or Urge Surfing (Khóa Quyết định 48 Giờ hoặc Lướt sóng Xung động) — Zone: `Closing`
- If the urge involves a major financial, career, or relationship decision during elevated mood (`moodPolarity >= +1`), lock it directly into the **48-Hour Impulse Vault** (`outcome: impulse-locked-48h`).
- Select 1 concrete bridge skill (`TIPP`, `Urge Surfing`, or `ACCEPTS`) for the next 20 minutes until the wave crests and subsides.
