# CBT Worry Postponement & Worry Box Protocol (worry-postponement)

## Clinical Mechanism
In Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD), trying to suppress a worry (*"Stop thinking about it!"*) creates an ironic rebound effect, while engaging with it immediately reinforces the metacognitive belief that *"Worrying keeps me safe"*. Postponing worry breaks the stimulus-response loop while respecting the mind's concern.

## Structured Clinical Steps

### Step 1: Catch & Deposit into the Worry Box (Bắt giữ & Gửi vào Hộp Lo âu) — Zone: `Supportive`
- Write down the intrusive "What-if" thought in 1 concise sentence.
- Acknowledge its presence without fighting it or solving it right now: *"Mình ghi nhận nỗi lo này đã xuất hiện và cất nó vào Hộp Lo âu đến giờ hẹn."*

### Step 2: Schedule the 15-Minute Worry Window (Hẹn Khung giờ Lo lắng Cố định 15 Phút) — Zone: `Supportive`
- Assign a consistent daily 15-minute window (e.g., `17:30–17:45`), at least **3 hours before bedtime** so it does not trigger insomnia.
- Return immediately to present-moment sensory reality.

### Step 3: Scheduled Review — Productive vs. Hypothetical Worry Triage (Phân loại Lo lắng lúc Mở Hộp) — Zone: `Core`
When the scheduled 15-minute window arrives, review each item in the Worry Box:
1. **Already Resolved / Faded**: Many worries lose 50–80% of their urgency by evening. Mark as resolved.
2. **Productive / Actionable Worry (Lo lắng Thực tế Có thể Hành động)**:
   - Criteria: There is a concrete physical action I can take today or tomorrow (e.g., paying a bill, booking a doctor appointment, preparing slides).
   - Action: Convert into **1 specific 5-minute next step** on the calendar.
3. **Hypothetical / Uncontrollable "What-If" Worry (Lo lắng Giả định Không thể Kiểm soát)**:
   - Criteria: Future uncertainty where no immediate action exists (*"What if the economy crashes in 3 years?"*, *"What if people secretly judge me?"*).
   - Action: Route to **Decatastrophizing / Prediction Calibration** or **DBT Radical Acceptance & Urge Surfing**.

### Step 4: Hard Stop at 15 Minutes (Đóng Hộp Đúng Giờ) — Zone: `Closing`
- When the 15-minute timer ends, close the Worry Box and switch to a physical grounding activity. Any remaining worries wait until tomorrow's window.
