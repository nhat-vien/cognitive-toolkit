# Pleasure-Predicting & Anti-Procrastination Sheet (pleasure-predicting)

## Clinical Mechanism
Depressive and anxious procrastination are fueled by two cognitive distortions:
1. **Fortune Telling on Satisfaction**: *"Even if I do this activity alone or start this task, I will feel 0% pleasure and 100% miserable."*
2. **Magnification of Task Difficulty**: *"This task will be 95% overwhelming and unbearable."*
Testing these predictions empirically as a no-lose scientific experiment shatters the *"Motivation must come before action"* myth.

## Structured Clinical Steps

### Step 1: Break into Micro-Steps & Schedule (Chia nhỏ thành Bước 5–15 Phút) — Zone: `Supportive`
- Choose 1 avoided task or potentially rewarding solo/social activity and break it into a concrete 5–15 minute segment.

### Step 2: Pre-Activity Predictions (Dự đoán Trước khi Làm) — Zone: `Core`
- Rate prior to starting:
  - **Predicted Difficulty (`0–100%`)**
  - **Predicted Pleasure / Satisfaction (`0–100%`)** (or `0–10` Pleasure `P` & Mastery `M`)

### Step 3: Execute the 5-Minute Experiment (Thực nghiệm 5 Phút Đầu tiên) — Zone: `Core`
- Perform just the agreed micro-step while observing thoughts without judgment.

### Step 4: Post-Activity Actual Ratings & Calibration (Chấm điểm Thực tế & Hiệu chỉnh Nhận thức) — Zone: `Closing`
- Record **Actual Difficulty (`0–100%`)** and **Actual Pleasure / Satisfaction (`0–100%`)**.
- Compute the **Prediction Error Delta**: Most users discover tasks are significantly less difficult (e.g., Predicted 90% vs. Actual 25%) and far more satisfying (Predicted 10% vs. Actual 65%) than their depressive mind claimed.
