# DBT Opposite Action Protocol (opposite-action)

## Clinical Principle
Every emotion carries a hardwired biological **Action Urge**:
- *Fear / Anxiety* → Urge to avoid, run away, or use safety behaviors.
- *Depression / Sadness* → Urge to withdraw, stay in bed, isolate, shut the curtains.
- *Anger* → Urge to attack, yell, send hostile messages.
- *Shame* → Urge to hide, disappear, avoid eye contact.
- *Guilt* → Urge to over-apologize or self-punish.
- *Hypomanic Euphoria* → Urge to overspend, take reckless risks, stay awake all night.

When the emotion or its intensity/duration **does NOT fit the objective facts**, acting opposite **100% all the way** (posture, facial expression, tone, and behavior) rewires the emotional response.

## Structured Clinical Steps

### Step 1: Name the Primary Emotion & Intensity (Gọi tên Cảm xúc Chủ đạo) — Zone: `Supportive`
- Identify the dominant emotion driving the body right now and rate intensity (`0–10`).

### Step 2: Pinpoint the Action Urge (Xác định Thôi thúc Hành động) — Zone: `Core`
- Ask: *"Cảm xúc này đang xúi giục cơ thể bạn làm hành động cụ thể gì ngay lúc này?"* (e.g., cancel the meeting, stay under the blanket, send an angry text, buy an expensive gadget at 2 AM).

### Step 3: The Justified Check — Dialectical Safety Pivot (Cổng Kiểm tra Sự thật Khách quan / Justified Check) — Zone: `Core` / `Closing`
- **Check the Facts**: Does the emotion and its action urge fit the objective facts of the current situation?
- **Branch A — Real-World Danger / Objectively Threatening Situation (`outcome: justified-exit`)**:
  - If fear is responding to actual physical danger, domestic abuse, stalking, or an unsafe environment, **turn Pushback `off` immediately**.
  - Validate that fear and avoidance/escape are 100% justified and protective. Switch to practical safety planning and close with `outcome: justified-exit`.
- **Branch B — Justified Emotion in Normal Context**:
  - E.g., healthy sadness after a real loss, or valid guilt after genuinely hurting someone.
  - Guide **Skillful Action / Problem-Solving / Repair** (e.g., make one sincere repair for valid guilt, then stop self-flagellating).
- **Branch C — Unjustified or Disproportionate Action Urge**:
  - E.g., avoiding a safe social presentation out of anxiety, isolating in bed from depressive inertia, or chasing a reckless hypomanic urge. Proceed to **Step 4**.

### Step 4: Act Opposite All The Way (Làm Ngược lại 100% Toàn diện) — Zone: `Core`
- Define **one concrete opposite action** executable within the next **10 to 60 minutes**:
  - *For Unjustified Fear*: Approach the avoided task gently, drop shoulders, unclench hands (**Willing Hands**).
  - *For Depressive Withdrawal*: Open the curtains, sit up, step outside for 5 minutes, send 1 brief text to a safe friend.
  - *For Unjustified Anger*: Step away from the keyboard, gently avoid attacking, practice perspective-taking.
  - *For Unjustified Shame*: Share the honest struggle with one safe person, keep head and eyes level.
  - *For Hypomanic Activation*: Dim the lights, put the phone/credit card in another room, lie down in quiet Dark Rest.
