# DBT ABC PLEASE Resilience & Biological Regulation Protocol (abc-please)

## Clinical Mechanism
Emotional dysregulation, depressive relapse, and bipolar mood switches rarely happen in a vacuum—they are amplified by biological vulnerabilities (**PLEASE**) and eroded positive reinforcement (**ABC**).

## Structured Clinical Steps

### Step 1: A — Accumulate Positive Experiences (Tích lũy Trải nghiệm Tích cực Ngắn & Dài hạn) — Zone: `Supportive`
- **Short-Term**: Schedule 1 small pleasant sensory or restorative moment today (even 3 minutes of warm tea, sunlight, or music).
- **Long-Term**: Identify 1 value-aligned step toward a life worth living.

### Step 2: B — Build Mastery (Xây dựng Cảm giác Tự chủ Mỗi ngày) — Zone: `Core`
- Plan 1 small challenge today that feels slightly stretching but achievable (`Confidence >= 7/10`), reinforcing self-efficacy against helplessness.

### Step 3: C — Cope Ahead for High-Risk Situations (Diễn tập Tâm trí Đối phó Trước Tình huống Khó) — Zone: `Core`
- Name an upcoming stressful or triggering event, predict the exact emotional urge that may arise, and mentally rehearse practicing your chosen CBT/DBT skill calmly from start to finish.

### Step 4: PLEASE — Biological Vulnerability Checklist (Lá chắn Sinh học PLEASE & Giấc ngủ Lưỡng cực) — Zone: `Supportive`
Audit and select 1 biological anchor to protect in the next 12 hours:
1. **PL — Treat PhysicaL Illness**: Take prescribed medications consistently (especially mood stabilizers in Bipolar Disorder—never self-discontinue).
2. **E — Balanced Eating**: Avoid blood-sugar crashes or skipping meals that mimic anxiety/fatigue.
3. **A — Avoid Mood-Altering Substances**: Eliminate alcohol, illicit substances, and late-day high-dose caffeine (major triggers for bipolar manic switches and panic attacks).
4. **S — Balanced Sleep (IPSRT Anchor)**: Protect consistent wake time and bedtime (`7–9 hours`; red flag if `< 5 hours` with elevated energy).
5. **E — Get Exercise**: Gentle daily movement (10–20 minutes) matched to current energy polarity.
